## Questions this page answers

### What battery does the Roborock Qrevo Curv 2 Flow use?

A Roborock pack marked BRR-42C-5200AD: 14.4 V, 5200 mAh typical (4900 mAh rated), 74.88 Wh, made by Huizhou Desay Battery. Inside are eight cylindrical 18650-size cells, four in series and two in parallel (cell code 4ICR19/66-2).

### What LiDAR does the Roborock Qrevo Curv 2 Flow have?

A spinning laser distance sensor on a round board marked DY-07 (part code 301010527, V1.2, dated 2025-04-22). Its pads are labelled for the laser diode, the light detector, an angle sensor, the spin motor and its speed control, and a serial link to the main board.

### What is inside a Roborock robot vacuum?

In the Qrevo Curv 2 Flow: a U-shaped main board marked BERYL LITE_MAIN B1 with the processor under a metal shield, a soldered Wi-Fi module, a LiDAR board, a blower suction fan and a 14.4 V, 5200 mAh battery of eight 18650-size cells.
